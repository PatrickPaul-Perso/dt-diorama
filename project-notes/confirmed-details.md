# Confirmed diorama details

This file is the working content reference for the website. Approximate values are identified as such.

## Purpose and audience

- Originally created as a Digital Technologies research demonstration platform.
- Intended to make data analytics, natural language processing, sentiment analysis, Industrial IoT, train logistics, manufacturing, Industry 4.0, automation, artificial intelligence, computer vision, and predictive analytics tangible to visitors.
- Potential recipients include museums, academia, model railway organizations, and enthusiasts able to receive and operate a large installation.

## Academic development

- The platform had an academic development and demonstration role in addition to its public-facing research purpose.
- Multiple undergraduate students worked on different aspects of the platform over successive academic terms.
- The diorama provided a shared physical environment for developing, integrating, testing, and demonstrating student work.
- Students communicated term results in conference-style presentations, connecting hands-on development with formal knowledge sharing.

## Physical system

- HO-scale railway diorama with a high level of scenic detail.
- Approximately 30 numbered modular landscape sections.
- Each module has a matching wooden transport crate.
- Elevated landscape features are stacked modules sized to pass through standard doors.
- A purpose-built dismantlable table is required to maintain flatness and module alignment.
- The table consists of two main sections.
- Each table section has four corner legs on casters and a fifth wheeled leg at its centre, for ten wheeled legs total.
- Three additional legs support the central span where the two table sections meet.

## Railway operation

- Digitrax DCC control system.
- VIA Rail passenger locomotive and CN freight locomotive with lights and sound.
- Passenger and freight rolling stock, including 3D-printed containers.
- Figure-eight-like two-level route with a large loop, passing tracks, a descent beneath the hotel, and a port-side rail yard.
- The last verified test allowed train operation across the complete layout.
- Turnouts are installed, but Tortoise motor programming remains to be completed.

## Scenic areas

- Ottawa-inspired passenger station.
- Mountain area representing autumn and winter, with tunnels, bridge, chalet, and ski lift.
- Montréal-inspired urban area with a station beneath the hotel.
- Montréal-inspired port area without a ship, including a rail yard and container lift.
- Prairie area with grain elevator, wheat field, and farm building.
- Industrial district with liquid storage tanks.
- Port water represents the river.

## Transfer and limitations

- The complete modular diorama, custom table, crates, DCC equipment, locomotives, and rolling stock form the transfer package.
- Computers, research sensors, cameras, and networking equipment are not included.
- The ski lift is the most fragile scenic element and requires careful handling.
- The exact module and rolling-stock inventory should be confirmed before a formal transfer agreement.
